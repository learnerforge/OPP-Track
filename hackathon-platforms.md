## How to Use This List

1. **For a hackathon tracker bot:** Focus on platforms with Public APIs (Kaggle, Codeforces, DoraHacks, Devpost, Challenge.gov) and Scrapable platforms (Devfolio, Unstop, ETHGlobal, lablab.ai).
2. **For job seekers:** Prioritize Corporate portals (Amazon, Microsoft, Flipkart), HackerEarth (hiring challenges), Unstop.
3. **For students:** MLH, Devpost, SIH, Imagine Cup, GSoC, picoCTF.
4. **For Web3 builders:** ETHGlobal, Colosseum, DoraHacks, Superteam Earn.
5. **For data scientists:** Kaggle, Analytics Vidhya, DrivenData, Zindi.
6. **For competitive programmers:** Codeforces, LeetCode, AtCoder, CodeChef.
